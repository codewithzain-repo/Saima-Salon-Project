Privacy policy.

A plain-language overview of the current website’s appointment form.

Last reviewed: October 2026

This policy describes the current frontend-only website. The business should review it before publication to ensure it matches actual practices and applicable legal requirements.

Appointment requests

The appointment form validates details in your browser and prepares a message for WhatsApp. When you continue, WhatsApp opens with the message pre-filled. The website itself does not submit the form to a server or save appointment details in a website database.

Information you choose to share

If you send the message through WhatsApp or call the salon, information is handled through those communication services and by the recipient. Review WhatsApp’s privacy information for details about its service.

Third-party resources

This website may load fonts and illustrative photographs from third-party providers. Those providers may process technical information when your browser requests their resources.

Changes and contact

This policy may need updating as the website and business practices evolve. The current site does not provide user accounts, payment processing, a booking database, or an admin dashboard. For salon questions, call 0300 1555831.
